03 — Projects

Selected work

Mini-project · Phylogenetics

Tracing the Phylogenetic Footprint of the Human Nervous System

Comparative genomic analysis of 10 major neurotransmission pathways across 17 taxa spanning archaea to vertebrates. Performed sequence homology assessment and phylogenetic reconstruction to investigate conservation of neurotransmitter biosynthetic enzymes, transporters and receptor subunits.

Identified conserved core signaling components in bilaterians, including GAD1, SLC6A4 and HTR2A, alongside lineage-specific receptor diversification.

Research internship · Computational drug discovery

Phytochemicals as Potential Inhibitors of Mutant Huntingtin Aggregation

At the Bioinformatics Centre, Madras Veterinary College (TANUVAS), investigated phytochemicals from Juglans regia as potential inhibitors of mutant huntingtin protein aggregation.

Screened 209 phytochemicals using SwissADME and ProTox-3.0, predicted pharmacodynamic activity with PASS, performed docking of 6 shortlisted compounds using AutoDock Vina and LibDock, and investigated complex stability using OpenMM.

Lead compound identified: Geranic acid.

Personal project · Machine learning

BioHTT — ML-Based Bioactivity Predictor for Huntingtin Protein

Developed and deployed a Streamlit web application for Active/Inactive compound bioactivity prediction against the Huntingtin protein. Processed SMILES into 2048-bit Morgan fingerprints (ECFP4) using RDKit and trained a Random Forest classifier with confidence scores. Integrated QSAR descriptor calculation into the application.
